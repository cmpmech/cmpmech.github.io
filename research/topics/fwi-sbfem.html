Full waveform inversion via the scaled-boundary FEM

To advance non-destructive evaluation and flaw detection in complex structures, we present a novel scaled boundary finite element formulation for time-domain wave propagation in inhomogeneous media. By lifting the conventional assumption of constant material properties along the radial coordinate within elements and employing finite differences to resolve spatial variations, the computational load is effectively decomposed into independent local problems and a single coupled global system.

The derivation establishes a scaled boundary semi-weak form of the scalar wave equation in heterogeneous media via the Galerkin method, integrated with the adjoint approach for time-domain full-waveform inversion. By incorporating an algorithmic condensation procedure to compute dynamic stiffness matrices on the fly and utilizing a limited-memory BFGS optimization algorithm, the framework achieves exceptional reconstruction quality and scalability.
